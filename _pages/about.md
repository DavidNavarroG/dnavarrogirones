Hello! I’m Dr. David Navarro-Gironés, a cosmologist and postdoctoral researcher at [Leiden Observatory](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/astronomy). My research focuses on weak gravitational lensing, which lets us map dark matter and test dark energy by measuring tiny distortions in galaxy shapes. However, galaxies also align intrinsically with their surroundings, mimicking part of the weak lensing signal. I work on measuring and modelling these intrinsic alignments, and on the photometric redshifts needed to do so, using data from current and upcoming cosmological surveys.

I grew up in Catalonia, where I studied Physics and Chemistry (UAB) and an MSc in Astrophysics, Cosmology and Particle Physics (UB). I did my PhD at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC) in Barcelona, advised by Dr. Martin Crocce and Dr. Enrique Gaztañaga, and defended it in 2024.

I currently lead a key intrinsic alignment analysis for Euclid DR1 and I'm a developer of the theory and likelihood tools behind it ([IATheory](https://github.com/DavidNavarroG/IATheory), [cloelib](https://github.com/cloe-org/cloelib), [CLOE](https://github.com/cloe-org/CLOE)). Before that, I led the photometric redshift and intrinsic alignment analyses of the PAU Survey.  I am a member of the [Euclid](https://www.euclid-ec.org/), [PAUS](https://www.pausurvey.org/), and [LSST DESC](https://lsstdesc.org/) collaborations.
